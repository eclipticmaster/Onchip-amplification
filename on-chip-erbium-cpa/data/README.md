# Material data (to be measured or obtained from authorized sources)

This folder is intentionally empty of data. To build a physical Er:Al₂O₃ gain model, you will eventually need:

- complex refractive index vs wavelength for the deposited Er:Al₂O₃ film, SiN and oxide;
- erbium absorption and emission cross-section spectra, preferably near 980 nm pump and across the signal band;
- Er concentration, excited-state lifetime and upconversion parameters;
- measured optical propagation loss and photoluminescence/gain data.

Document units, temperature, fabrication process, measurement method and source for every dataset. Put proprietary files in `data/private/` (ignored by Git), and only commit datasets that you are permitted to distribute.
